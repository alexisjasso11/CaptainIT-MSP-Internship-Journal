## What I Learned
Having already worked inside Entra ID during the MFA audit made today's studying noticeably easier — the portal, the tenant structure, and the admin workflows were already familiar. The biggest conceptual takeaway was understanding the three identity models clearly: **on-premises AD** (all local, physical infrastructure dependent), **cloud-only Entra ID** (fully managed in Azure, no on-prem requirement), and **hybrid** (both connected via Entra Connect, syncing on-prem AD to the cloud). For an MSP like Captain IT managing dozens of client tenants, Entra ID's cloud-native approach removes a huge amount of infrastructure overhead compared to managing individual on-premises domain controllers for every client. That's a direct connection between what I've been doing all internship and where the industry is heading.
